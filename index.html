About DRHC

Healthcare built around attention, expertise and trust.

Established in 2014 by founding Director Dr. Rami Hamed, DRHC is a multispecialty day-case hospital in Dubai. Its stated mission is to raise patients' hopes and gain their trust through attentive, responsible healthcare.

Helpful information

Visit the official FAQs →
Where is Dr. Rami Hamed Center located?

DRHC lists two Dubai locations: Building 52, Dubai Healthcare City, and Tulip Oasis 9, Wadi Al Safa 3, Majan, Dubai.

How can I request an appointment?

Contact the appointment team at +971 4 279 8200 or info@drhc.ae to ask about appointment arrangements.

Which medical specialties are listed by DRHC?

The official specialties directory includes areas such as orthopedics, spinal surgery, cardiology, dermatology, gastroenterology, general medicine, general surgery, ophthalmology, pediatrics, urology and more.

Does DRHC list diagnostic imaging services?

Yes. DRHC's about page lists CT scan, MRI, X-ray, bone densitometry, mammography and ultrasound, along with laboratory and day-case operating facilities. Confirm specific service availability with the centre.

How can I confirm opening hours?

Contact DRHC before your visit. Opening-hour information differs between some of the centre's official pages, so the relevant branch should confirm its current schedule.
