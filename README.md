# Dr. Rami Hamed Center (DRHC) – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Dr. Rami Hamed Center (DRHC) is a Dubai-based multispecialty medical center and day-case hospital established in 2014 by Dr. Rami Hamed.

The center provides general medical consultations, specialist treatments, diagnostic imaging, laboratory services and day-case surgical procedures across two Dubai locations: Dubai Healthcare City and Majan.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting DRHC as a comprehensive healthcare destination, improving specialty discovery, making doctor information easier to navigate and providing a clearer path to appointments and branch information.

## 2. Three Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging
- Introduces a clearer headline communicating DRHC's multispecialty healthcare offering.
- Highlights the combination of medical consultations, specialist expertise, diagnostics and surgical services.
- Gives visitors quicker access to the center's key healthcare offerings.

**Problem addressed:** The previous homepage did not communicate the full range of DRHC's healthcare services clearly enough at first glance.

### 2. Better Organisation of Specialties and Doctors
- Structures medical specialties into clearer, patient-focused categories.
- Improves the presentation of doctors and their professional information.
- Makes it easier for visitors to identify relevant departments and explore available medical services.

**Problem addressed:** The previous presentation of specialties and doctor information lacked a sufficiently clear structure, making it harder for patients to find the appropriate specialist.

### 3. Clearer Appointment and Location Experience
- Gives appointment and telephone contact options greater prominence.
- Presents the two medical center locations separately and more clearly.
- Improves access to branch-specific information.
- Creates a more direct journey from discovering a medical service to contacting the center.

**Problem addressed:** Appointment information and branch details were fragmented, making it harder for visitors to identify the right location and take the next step.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Establishes a professional healthcare-focused presentation that reflects DRHC's multispecialty positioning.
- **Layout:** Creates a clearer hierarchy for the introduction, medical specialties, doctor information, facilities and branch locations.
- **User Experience:** Simplifies specialty discovery and navigation towards relevant medical information.
- **Content Consistency:** Organises department, doctor and location information into a more consistent structure.
- **Conversion Focus:** Makes appointment and contact actions easier to identify throughout the homepage.
- **Overall Appearance:** Creates a more structured and cohesive digital presentation of DRHC's medical services and facilities.
